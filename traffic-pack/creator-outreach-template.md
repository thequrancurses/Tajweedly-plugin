## Follow-up (after 5 days, once only)

Subject: Re: Free Tajweed sessions for your community

Assalamu alaikum {FirstName}, just a gentle follow-up on my note below. Happy to send the free lesson link whenever suits you. If this is not a fit, no worries at all, and I will not email again.

## Rules for yourself
- One email per creator, one follow-up maximum, then stop.
- Use only the business email shown on their profile/channel "About" page, or their official contact form / DM if they say DMs are open.
- Never email them from scraped or purchased lists; always include who you are and a real way to reach you.
- Track replies in a sheet: creator, platform, followers, date sent, reply, status.
