# Outreach pack (free, legal, no scraping)

Rules: only contact organisations through **their public contact form or the public business email they publish**, one personalised message each, honour any "no" immediately. Never buy lists, never message personal numbers. Replace every [PLACEHOLDER]. No names or emails are provided here on purpose: find them yourself on each organisation's own website.

## 1. Mosques and weekend Islamic schools (UK/US)
**Where to find them:** Google Maps ("mosque near [city]"), the Muslim Council of Britain / ISNA public directories, each mosque's own "contact" page.

**Message (short):**
> Assalamu alaikum [NAME/Team],
> I'm [YOUR NAME] from Tajweedly. We built a free 3-minute Tajweed level test (beginner / intermediate / advanced, plus a kids readiness check). Many of your students/parents may find it useful to know where to start.
> You are welcome to link it or embed it on your site for free: [EMBED PAGE LINK]. No sign-up is needed to take the test.
> If you ever need a free sample class for a parent, just reply. JazakAllahu khayran, [YOUR NAME]

## 2. Guest post pitch (Muslim blogs / parenting sites)
> Subject: Guest article idea: "[TITLE]" for [SITE]
> Salam [NAME], I read your piece "[THEIR ARTICLE]". I'd like to offer an original article, "[TITLE]" (800-1200 words), written for your readers, with a short author bio only. Outline: [3 bullets]. Happy to adapt to your style. [YOUR NAME], Tajweedly.com

Ideas for titles: "5 Tajweed mistakes adults make when learning to read Quran", "How to know if your child is ready to start Quran reading", "Noon sakinah rules explained in one page".

## 3. Data-PR pitch (only after the plugin holds >=30 attempts per level)
Use Insights > press summary inside the plugin. It only contains real numbers.
> Subject: New data: the Tajweed question most Muslims get wrong
> Hi [NAME], from [N] anonymous online Tajweed tests we found that [PASTE PRESS SUMMARY LINE]. Charts and the full table available. [YOUR NAME]

## 4. Communities (Quora, Reddit, Facebook groups)
- Answer real questions with a full useful answer first; mention the test only if it fits, once, no repeated links.
- Read each community's rules; many forbid self-promotion. Disclose that you work with Tajweedly.
- Never post the same text in several places.

## 5. Directories and listings (free, to find yourself)
Google Business Profile (if you have a legal presence), Trustpilot / Google reviews from real students (ask, never write), online-Quran-class directories, Muslim business directories in the UK/US, university Islamic society pages, Wikipedia is NOT for promotion.
